# Official EU Legal Sources

**Verified:** 24 August 2026  
**Scope:** GDPR, DPIA, international transfers, AI Act, ePrivacy, Data Act and the Digital Omnibus status relevant to the lab.

## GDPR

1. European Union, [Regulation (EU) 2016/679, General Data Protection Regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).
   - Article 4: definitions
   - Article 5: principles
   - Article 6: lawful bases
   - Article 9: special-category data
   - Articles 13 to 22: data-subject information and rights
   - Article 28: processor contracts
   - Article 32: security
   - Article 35: DPIA
   - Articles 44 to 49: international transfers

2. European Data Protection Board, [Guidance on DPIAs and high-risk processing](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/documents/guideline/data-protection-impact-assessments-high-risk-processing_en).

3. European Data Protection Board, [International data transfers](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/sme/be-compliant/international-data-transfers_en).

4. European Commission, [Standard Contractual Clauses](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/standard-contractual-clauses-scc_en).

5. European Commission, [EU-US data transfers and the Data Privacy Framework](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/international-dimension-data-protection/eu-us-data-transfers_en).

8. European Commission, [Guidelines on AI Act transparency obligations](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-ai-transparency-obligations).

## ePrivacy

9. European Union, [Directive 2002/58/EC on privacy and electronic communications](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj/eng).

National implementing law and regulator guidance must also be checked for the country in which terminal access, communications or tracking occurs.

10. European Commission, [Data Act explained](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/data-act-explained).

11. European Commission, [Data Act policy page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/data-act).

## Digital Omnibus status

12. European Commission, [Simplification and implementation tracker](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-making-process/better-regulation/simplification-implementation-and-enforcement/simplification_en).

As of the verification date, the AI simplification component has been adopted, while the broader digital-legislation proposal remains in the co-legislative process. Proposed GDPR, ePrivacy and Data Act changes must not be treated as current law until formally adopted and applicable.

## Citation approach

The audit uses official EU and EDPB sources as the primary legal references. Vendor claims, marketing pages and unsourced summaries are not treated as evidence of legal compliance, hosting location, retention or transfer safeguards.
